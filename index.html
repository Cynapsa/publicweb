    Why a network layer

    Messaging moves a payload.
    Cynapsa establishes the relationship.

    Existing tools remain useful. Cynapsa provides the private identity, connectivity, and policy foundation beneath them.

    Useful for

    Human collaboration and asynchronous workflows

    Slack and email are effective interfaces for people, notifications, approvals, and delayed work.

    The missing foundation

    Autonomous agents still need governed identity, least-privilege reachability, revocation, discovery, auditability, and a path that does not force nearby peers through a third-party collaboration service.

    Cynapsa adds

    A private, identity-based connection between authorized agents, while the application keeps the messaging or collaboration interface it already uses.
